I'm a Senior Machine Learning Scientist at Matterworks, a biotech startup in Boston applying AI to mass spectrometry data. I joined as the company's third ML researcher shortly after seed and have since helped grow the ML team through Series A, working across model development, deployment, and technical leadership.

My work spans:

- **Concentration prediction models** — SMILES-based molecular graph priors, OOD/uncertainty estimation
- **LSM-MS2**, a transformer-based retrieval system for lipid identification achieving state-of-the-art results on the MassSpecGym benchmark ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26715))
- A **large-scale multimodal foundation model** over MS1/MS2/metadata, trained with 8-GPU model parallelism

Outside of my primary work, I do independent research in physics-informed machine learning and out-of-distribution detection — see [Publications](/publications/).

My background spans ML research, automotive AI (Ford), academic research (University of Wisconsin, University of Michigan), and energy/process engineering (ExxonMobil).
