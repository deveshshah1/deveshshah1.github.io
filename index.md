I am a Senior Machine Learning Scientist at [Matterworks](https://www.matterworks.ai), where I build foundation models for mass spectrometry. Previously, I was a Machine Learning Research Scientist at Ford Research & Advanced Engineering, and before that a process modeling engineer at [ExxonMobil](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com). I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the [University of Wisconsin - Madison](https://www.cs.wisc.edu) and my B.S.E. in Chemical Engineering from the [University of Michigan](https://che.engin.umich.edu).

My work sits at the intersection of **AI × Science**: building, scaling, and deploying models that learn from messy, high-dimensional scientific data. At Matterworks, I developed the core architecture and self-supervised pretraining for the Large Spectral Model (LSM), and led the development of [LSM-MS2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26715), which achieves state-of-the-art results on the MassSpecGym benchmark. I joined as the third ML researcher after the seed round and helped grow the research team from 3 to 8 through Series A. My research interests include:

- *Foundation models for scientific data:* Multimodal pretraining over spectra, molecules, and metadata at the scale of millions of samples and 100M-point inputs.
- *Representation learning:* Contrastive and curriculum learning strategies that separate structurally similar molecules in embedding space.
- *Physics-informed machine learning:* Understanding when and why physics-informed neural networks fail, and how training interventions interact.
- *Reliability in the wild:* Out-of-distribution detection and uncertainty estimation beyond toy benchmarks.
